Sheffield DevOps

Sheffield DevOps is an online meetup with the aim of showcasing exciting DevOps, SRE, and Platform Engineering (and adjacent) practices in the region. We are open to talks from practitioners from Sheffield and the wider area, but also regularly host talks from further afield

Our events are free to attend, and open to anyone regardless of experience or background

Attend

Meetups are a great way to learn. This might be a new technology, or from the experiences of others. It’s also an opportunity to share your thoughts and find others that are having similar problems and solutions. You’ll also find out what DevOps related projects and activities companies are doing in Sheffield. It might even lead to some interesting career options

Speak

We are always looking for speakers to talk at our meetups about DevOps and related topics. This could also be a tutorial on using a technology, or a case study sharing experiences and lessons learned. We’re pretty flexible on the format, so let us know if you want to do something other than a 40 minute talk

Conduct

Meetups are meant to be fun. People from different social backgrounds, industries and technologies attend, so when you are talking to people please consider what you say and how you speak. Basically, be nice, be friendly, and be respectful. Our full Code of Conduct is available here
